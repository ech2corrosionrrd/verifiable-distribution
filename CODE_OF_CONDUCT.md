# Code of Conduct


## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming bad intent.
- Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Welcome newcomers and people writing in a second language (we work in English and Ukrainian).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks and trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spam, promotion of scams, token shilling or unsolicited advertising in issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and to public spaces when someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

To report a violation, email **w3ir@pm.me**. Reports are handled confidentially, and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can. If the report concerns a maintainer, state this in the email and we will arrange for another maintainer or an outside person to review it.
